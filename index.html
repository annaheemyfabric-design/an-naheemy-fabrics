FABRIC MAINTENANCE GUIDE

How to Care for Your AN NAHEEMY Fabrics

Maximize longevity, color richness, and crease performance for Cashmere, Wool, and Linen.

Cashmere & Virgin Wool

Dry clean recommended. When ironing, use low-to-medium steam setting with a pressing cloth to preserve fiber softness.

7/10 Star Suiting

Hang on padded suit hangers. Avoid harsh wringing. Holds crisp iron creases for Senator and Agbada cuts.

German Wool & Jonkoso

Durable weave structure. Machine wash on gentle cold cycle or dry clean. Iron while slightly damp for razor-sharp edges.

Irish Linen Material

Highly breathable. Warm water wash and high steam iron. Air dry flat to retain natural textile texture.
